Setu

This page needs its key

Open the address setu serve printed, the one ending in #token=…. The page keeps the key after that. Someone else's folder? Ask for your link in your chat: /accounts page.

Your connections

Each one is a site you signed in to. Agents you allow use it through Setu; none of them ever sees the key.

Available to connect

Installed here, not signed in to yet. Pick what agents may do with it, then Connect. You can change the level later, and Disconnect revokes the key.

Add a site

A site with no connector: a window opens on it here, you sign in, and Setu writes careful rules for it. Agents can read it; anything that would buy or pay is handed back to you.

People's pages

Who has their own Setu page open, on what, and since when. A person gets a link in their chat (/accounts page); closing theirs here means they need a new one.

Catalog

Connectors Setu's signed catalog lists: who wrote each, how many people installed it, and any version taken back. Install checks the download against the hash shown.

Certifiers you trust

People who checked a version for themselves and signed what they found. Trusting a new one stays in the terminal (setu certify trust FILE.pub): it starts from a file you were handed.

Settings

What setu config remembers. None of it is a key.

No key is ever shown here, or sent to this page. Locking the folder stays in the terminal: setu lock.
